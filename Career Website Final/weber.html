Background

Weber

One of the most iconic American grill brands and is highly trusted in the grill/barbecue industry.

Weber's audience tends to be people who take grilling seriously and owns the cookout with the grill as their domain.

A premium brand without crossing into luxury — their reputation for craftsmanship and durability is core to their brand and equity.

Alexander Volkanovski

Australian MMA fighter and UFC featherweight champion, often considered one of the greatest.

Volk is a fan favorite because of his charismatic personality, family man image, and authenticity in a way that's rare from an elite professional fighter.

Hosts a cooking content series, "Cooking with Volk", which came from him cooking for his family and teammates.

The Opportunity

Weber haven't completely tapped into the younger market, but have made efforts to, pushing into the college tailgating space with licensed grills with universities and athletic programs.

Volk's cooking series, working class authenticity, and gritty MMA background makes him a natural brand fit with Weber.

The UFC has seen immense growth in the past decade with a significant spike in Gen Z viewership.

Main Insights

In an era when entertainment has become increasingly individual, sporting events like UFC fight nights have become opportunities for social gatherings where friends can eat, watch, and socialize

The Grill is Going Younger

Grilling isn't just for dads anymore with younger consumers claiming the grill and bringing their own culture with it.

Entertainment Went Solo

Streaming and social media have made entertainment a solo activity, but increasingly alone.

Sports Still Gather People

Sports are one of the last things people still watch together with UFC fight nights giving people a reason to gather, grill, and enjoy the night with each other.

Campaign Concept

Strategy and Positioning

The main goal of this campaign is for awareness and relevance with younger audiences.

Position Weber as the brand that fuels fight nights from individual experiences to shared rituals.

The campaign's success will be measured through brand sentiment shifts, UGC volume, and new customer acquisition in the 18-34 year old demographic.

Fire Up The Crew

The name works on two levels at once: firing up the grill and firing up the people around it.

"The crew" isn't just Volk's training team but everyone you call when the fight's on and the food's ready.

Volk's Role

Alex Volkanovski isn't going to be just a celebrity spokesperson but the embodiment of the campaign idea.

He lives the role of someone who feeds his crew, brings energy to a room, and is the guy people want to gather around.

Campaign Execution

"Fight Night with Volk" Content Series

Alexander Volkanovski hosts a series where he shows how he prepares for fight nights, partnering with Weber to fire up the crew. Full episodes on YouTube, clips on TikTok and Reels.

#FireUpTheCrew Social Campaign

Fans join the campaign by posting their own fight night setups, watch parties, and food spreads. Weber reposts the best ones and Volk reacts to fan content in dedicated videos.

The Live Experience

The campaign's live extension takes the backyard cookout concept to scale. The Backyard Arena at UFC Fight Week is the flagship moment and the Fire Up the Crew Tailgate Tour hit major US cities on UFC PPV weekends.

Measuring Success

Brand Awareness Among 18-34s

Tracked through brand sentiment surveys and aided awareness studies measuring Weber's recognition and perception within the UFC and combat sports demographic before, during, and after the campaign.

The goal is a measurable shift in how younger consumers perceive Weber (a "dad brand" to the brand that facilitates social connection).

Community Participation

Measured by the volume, reach, and quality of #FireUpTheCrew content across social media (hashtag reach, total post volume, engagement rates).

High quality UGC indicates the campaign has moved beyond advertising and has become something people genuinely want to participate in.

New Customer Acquisition

Tracked through first time Weber purchases among the 18-34 year old demographic during and following the campaign period.

The ultimate measure of whether cultural relevance translated into commercial impact.

Why It Works

The White Space

Weber has the brand equity, the product, and the heritage but they've been missing the younger generation who don't yet see grilling as part of their identity.

Fire Up The Crew is the entry point into that demographic, positioning Weber inside a world where their target audience already lives.

The Right Partner

Alex Volkanovski is a genuine representation of what the campaign aims to be: someone who enjoys cooking for his people, brings positive energy wherever he goes, and has built an audience that trusts him.

The Human Insight

Entertainment has gone solo with people watching more than ever, but increasingly alone.

The cookout and watch party are two of the few communal rituals that the algorithm hasn't touched, giving Weber a way to bring the crew together.

Cultural Case Study

IHOP x Malik Nabers

How a fantasy football punishment became a real campaign win

Background

Every year, millions of football fans compete against each other in fantasy football alongside the NFL season. To spice up the stakes, rewards and punishments are implemented around many leagues.

A popular punishment idea was for the last place player to sit in an IHOP for 24 hours, with each pancake eaten removing an hour.

Why Malik Nabers?

Most fans expected elite production from the Giants star receiver, but expectations were brutally crushed with his season-ending knee injury just a few weeks into the season.

With his unexpected injury, Nabers became synonymous with a doomed fantasy season.

What was the Goal?

"Fantasy football has turned into a cultural obsession rooted in friendly and intense competition, and we saw an opportunity to create real-time engagement by flipping those last-place blues into a winning experience for everyone."

— Lawrence Kim, CEO of IHOP

What Made it Work?

Humor

Malik Nabers embraces his role as the "fantasy bust" who cost many of his believers a full day of pancakes. The light-hearted humor plays into meme culture and rewards viewers for understanding the context and joke.

Playing into a Trend

IHOP was already a trendy spot for punishments, so the ad positioned them to be the solution for fantasy losers. Both Nabers and IHOP are self-aware of their online perception, making the ad feel like a cultural snapshot rather than a manufactured moment.

Simple Messaging

IHOP's Bottomless Pancakes deal gives fantasy losers a budget-friendly option and shows the audience they are present in the culture. The ad doesn't overstay its welcome or over-explain the joke, which makes it easy to share on social media.

Key Takeaways

Relatability and Self Awareness Wins

IHOP taps into a real viral trend and relates to people who have actually been frustrated with Nabers or fantasy football throughout the season.

Nabers going along with the bit is a breath of fresh air since athletes are commonly celebrated or hyped up in commercials.

The ad feels authentic instead of feeling like a corporate boardroom idea.

Participate in the Culture

The context is assumed and the inside joke isn't over explained, which makes IHOP feel like "one of us" to the audience.

Quick punchlines and funny situations lean into meme culture and make the ad easily clippable to share around.

The ad was perfectly timed during the start of NFL playoffs and the end of fantasy football season — the peak relevance of all things football.

Aligns with the Brand Perfectly

The concept of the ad and partnership with Malik Nabers truly feels like it was inevitable by how well it all works together.

The ad feels warm and comforting, which is exactly what IHOP positions itself as being.

Bottomless Pancakes flips a losing situation into a celebration, which reinforces IHOP as the place for fantasy punishments.
